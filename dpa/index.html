1. Scope and roles

This Addendum forms part of the Terms of Service between Hyperbyte Cloud (“Processor”) and the customer (“Controller”). It applies where we process personal data on your behalf in providing HyperbyteDB Cloud.

You are the controller and determine the purposes and means of processing. We are the processor and act only on your instructions. Where UK GDPR, EU GDPR or both apply, this Addendum is intended to satisfy Article 28.

2. Subject matter and details of processing

Subject matterProvision of a managed time-series database service
DurationThe term of the Terms of Service, plus the retention period in section 10
Nature and purposeStorage, indexing, querying, replication and backup of data you submit, in order to provide the service
Types of personal dataDetermined by you. Typically device, session or user identifiers within time-series measurements and tags, plus account and contact details of your authorised users
Categories of data subjectDetermined by you. Typically your end users, employees or the operators of devices you monitor
Special category dataNot expected. You must not submit special category data without agreeing additional measures with us in writing

3. Our obligations

We will:

  • Process personal data only on your documented instructions, including as set out in the Terms of Service and this Addendum.
  • Tell you if we believe an instruction breaches data protection law, and may suspend that processing until resolved.
  • Ensure personnel with access are bound by confidentiality and are trained appropriately, and limit access to those who need it.
  • Implement the technical and organisational measures in Annex A.
  • Assist you, at your cost where the effort is substantial, with data protection impact assessments and with consultations with a supervisory authority.
  • Not process personal data for our own purposes, and never sell it.

4. Your obligations

You confirm that you have a lawful basis for the data you submit, that you have provided any notices and obtained any consents required, and that your instructions to us comply with data protection law. You are responsible for the configuration choices you make, including retention periods and who you grant access to.

5. Sub-processors

6. International transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the UK or EEA to a country without an adequacy decision, that transfer is made under the International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses as applicable, together with any supplementary measures required following a transfer risk assessment.

7. Data subject requests

The service gives you the ability to access, correct, export and delete data within your instance, so that you can respond to data subject requests yourself. If a request reaches us directly, we will not respond to it substantively but will refer it to you without undue delay. Where you need assistance we cannot reasonably expect you to perform yourself, we will help, at your cost where the effort is substantial.

8. Personal data breaches

We will notify you without undue delay, and in any event within [TO CONFIRM — 48 or 72] hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting your data. The notification will describe the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate volume of data and data subjects affected so far as known, the likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed, and a contact point. We will provide further information as the investigation progresses, and will cooperate with your own notification obligations.

9. Audits and information

We will make available the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Addendum. You may audit our compliance no more than once in any 12-month period, on at least 30 days’ written notice, during business hours, subject to confidentiality, and without unreasonable disruption to our operations or access to other customers’ data. Where we hold a relevant third-party certification or audit report, providing it will satisfy an audit request. [TO CONFIRM] We currently hold no third-party certification; see section 12.

10. Deletion and return

On termination you may export your data while your account remains active and for [TO CONFIRM] days afterwards. After that we will delete it, including from backups within [TO CONFIRM] days, except where we are required by law to retain it. We will confirm deletion in writing on request.

11. Liability

Liability under this Addendum is subject to the limitations in the Terms of Service, except where data protection law does not permit such limitation.

12. Current certification status

We hold no SOC 2, ISO 27001 or equivalent certification at present. We would rather tell you that here than have you discover it during procurement. If a certification is a requirement for you, talk to us about timelines before you commit.
