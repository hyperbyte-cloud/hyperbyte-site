Pricing

Free to run. Shards to grow.

Self-hosting is free, with no feature gates. Cloud starts at one shard and scales to any number after that. Each shard is $299 a month: 4 CPU and 16GB. Disk is $0.20 per GB-month, for the storage you provision. Prices are USD.

Scale-out

Linear in shards. Linear in compute.

You choose any whole number of shards, from 1 upward. Throughput tracks that count. Compute does too: the bill is the count times $299. Disk is the other line: every gigabyte is $0.20 a month, on whichever shard holds it.

What the price is not

No enterprise tier for the basics.

Replication, retention, and continuous queries are in the open-source binary. Cloud does not charge extra to turn them on. You scale with two numbers: how many shards, and how many gigabytes.

Early access is a short form. A person reads it. The prices on this page are the prices.

  • +Same InfluxDB v1 endpoints on every shard.
  • +Add a shard, pay $299 again. Add a gigabyte, pay $0.20.
  • +Regions, a published uptime SLA, compliance certification, and bring-your-own-cloud are still ahead.

A bill, worked

One shard and 100 GB of disk is $299 + $20, so $319 a month. Four shards and 1 TB is $1,196 + $200, so $1,396 a month.
