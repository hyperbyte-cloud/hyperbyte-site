1. Who we are

Hyperbyte Cloud (“Hyperbyte”, “we”, “us”) provides HyperbyteDB, an open-source time-series database, and HyperbyteDB Cloud, a managed version of it.

[TO CONFIRM] Registered company name, company number and registered office address.

For any privacy question, or to exercise the rights described in section 8, contact hello@hyperbyte.cloud.

We are the controller for the personal data described in this policy. Where you use HyperbyteDB Cloud to store data about your own users, you are the controller of that data and we act as your processor — see our Data Processing Addendum.

2. What we collect

Website visitors

Standard request data — IP address, page requested, referring site, and browser user agent — is logged by our web server and by Cloudflare, for security and to keep the site running. We do not use analytics or advertising tools on this site. See section 5 on cookies.

Early access and contact enquiries

When you complete our early access form we collect your email address, and optionally your name, company, and the information you choose to give us about your current database, workload scale, data residency requirements and timescales. The form is operated by Typeform.

Customers

Account details (name, email, company, billing address), authentication credentials, billing and payment records, support correspondence, and operational logs and metrics relating to your use of the service.

Payments

Card details are collected and processed by our payment provider, not by us. We receive only the transaction record and the last four digits of the card. [TO CONFIRM] Name of payment provider.

5. Cookies

This website sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics. Fonts, scripts and images are served from this site, so viewing a page does not contact any other company.

The early access form is hosted by Typeform. When you open it, Typeform may set its own cookies; see Typeform’s privacy policy.

Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie to filter automated traffic. Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, strictly necessary cookies do not need consent. If you become a HyperbyteDB Cloud customer, the customer console may also set strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in.

6. International transfers

Some providers listed above process data outside the United Kingdom. Where they do, we rely on UK adequacy regulations, or on the International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, together with any additional safeguards required.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have data erased, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right not to be subject to solely automated decisions with legal effect — we do not make such decisions.

To exercise any of these, email hello@hyperbyte.cloud. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk, or call 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to address it first.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and least-privilege administrative access, encrypted backups, and logging of administrative activity. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data and presents a risk to you, we will notify you and the ICO as required.

10. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

11. Changes

If we change this policy materially we will update the date above and, for customers, notify you by email before the change takes effect.
