Changes

The ceiling, and the licence

  • Cardinality is capacity. Add a shard, add that capacity again
  • Replication, retention, and continuous queries are in the Apache 2.0 binary
  • Plan on 1.5M points/sec sustained, p95 under 250ms

Price

Free to run. Shards to grow.

Self-hosting is free, with no feature gates. Cloud is $299 a month per shard: 4 CPU and 16GB. Disk is $0.20 per GB-month. One shard and 100GB of disk is $319 a month. The pricing page has the rest of the table, and Cloud is the same database, operated for you.

Questions

Before you cut over.

Is HyperbyteDB a drop-in for InfluxDB v1?

Yes. It speaks line protocol and InfluxQL on the same /write and /query endpoints. Telegraf, Grafana, and existing clients stay. You change the URL. Coverage, and the differences, are written up on the compatibility page.

What does HyperbyteDB Cloud cost?

Self-hosting is free. Cloud compute is $299 a month per shard. A shard is 4 CPU and 16GB. Disk is $0.20 per GB-month. One shard and 100GB of disk is $319 a month.

How do I start?

Request early access for Cloud. The form is short. To self-host, pull ghcr.io/hyperbyte-cloud/hyperbytedb and listen on port 8086. No form.
