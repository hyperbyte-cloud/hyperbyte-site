Questions

Before you send the form.

Is Cloud a different API?

No. It speaks line protocol and InfluxQL on the same /write and /query endpoints as self-hosted HyperbyteDB.

What do you actually operate?

Tuning, replication, upgrades, backups, and capacity. You keep Telegraf, Grafana, and the queries.

What is the price?

$299 a month per shard, 4 CPU and 16GB. Disk is $0.20 per GB-month. Start at one shard and add any number after that. The pricing page has the full table. It is not quoted per deal.

What is still ahead?

Regions, a published uptime SLA, formal compliance certification, and bring-your-own-cloud. Write to an engineer if one of those is the reason you are waiting.
